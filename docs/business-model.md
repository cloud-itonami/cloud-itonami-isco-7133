# Business Model: Building Structure Cleaning Crew Scheduling & Logistics Coordination Service

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7133`
- ISCO-08: `7133`
- Occupation: Building Structure Cleaners
- Social impact: worker-safety, building-durability, local-jobs

## Scope

**This actor coordinates job-site scheduling and logistics only.** It
never performs building-cleaning work itself, never finalizes a
cleaning-execution decision (including a height-work go/no-go call),
and never overrides a site-safety officer's judgment. Building
Structure Cleaners wash windows and clean building exteriors/facades
at height on active job sites -- one of the highest fall-risk trades
-- and routinely use chemical cleaning agents, so every proposal this
actor's advisor can make is limited to coordination, not execution.

## Customer

- independent building-cleaning contractors
- window-washing and facade-cleaning crews and site foremen

## Offer

- work-record logging (task, materials usage, progress)
- crew/task scheduling proposals
- safety-concern surfacing (fall hazard, weather condition, equipment
  condition, chemical exposure)
- cleaning-materials/equipment supply-order coordination

## Revenue

- monthly coordination-platform retainer
- per-site logistics fee

## Trust Controls

- no cleaning-execution decision (including a height-work go/no-go
  call) is ever finalized by this actor
- no site-safety officer's judgment is ever overridden by this actor
- every safety-concern flag always escalates to human sign-off
- supply orders above the registered cost threshold always escalate
  to human sign-off
- job-site and crew-member provenance is independently verified
  before any coordination action
- coordination and audit records are auditable, not editable
